What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) operates directly on the front lines. While traditional software engineers build products in isolation, an FDE deploys, customizes, and integrates complex software and AI systems directly into the client's live enterprise environment. They sit at the rare intersection of highly technical software engineering, infrastructure architecture, and direct client-facing problem solving.

Engineering
Writing robust, production-level code to solve bespoke challenges.
Architecture
Designing scalable cloud infrastructure and deployment pipelines.
Consulting
Translating ambiguous client needs into concrete technical solutions.
